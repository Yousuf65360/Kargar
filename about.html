ABOUT KARGAR

Better neighborhoods start with action.

KARGAR exists to provide practical solutions for everyday urban and community problems.

OUR STORY

A private solution to everyday civic problems.

In many neighborhoods, simple problems can remain unresolved because public services may be delayed, unavailable or unable to respond quickly enough.

KARGAR was created around a simple idea: people should have access to a practical, organized way to request the work they need.

From cleaning and waste removal to maintenance and drainage support, KARGAR brings these everyday service requirements together on one platform.
